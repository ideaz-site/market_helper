# Market Helper privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Market Helper does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no server, no analytics and no accounts.

## What it stores

Everything you set up is saved on your own device with Chrome's local extension storage:

- the hostnames of the sites you add
- the profiles you create for each site
- the text fields you pick (a label and a CSS selector that locates the field)
- the text you type for each field
- the items in your catalog: title, price, quantity, condition, description, and the photos and videos you add

This data never leaves your browser. Removing a field, profile, site, item, photo or video deletes it, and uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

## What it accesses

- **Web pages.** When you click Pick a field or Paste, the extension runs on that page to highlight its text fields or to fill in your saved text. It also checks whether your saved fields are present on a saved site you have open. It does not read, record or send page content anywhere.
- **Clipboard.** If you paste into a field that has no saved text, the extension reads your clipboard once and puts that text into the field. Clipboard contents are not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Tab addresses.** The extension reads the address of the current tab to show which saved site you are on.
